Malte Baader

Behavioural & experimental economist

I'm a Research Data Manager at FAIR (Centre for Experimental Research on Fairness, Inequality and Rationality) at the Norwegian School of Economics (NHH) in Bergen. My research focuses on creativity, team cohesion and organisational innovation, and I build experimental tools for scientific research.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Zurich. I received my PhD from CeDEx at the University of Nottingham.
